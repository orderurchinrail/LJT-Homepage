# About Me

I am Junteng Liu, a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

## Academic Background
- **2024 – Present**: Ph.D. in Computer Science, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), Hong Kong
  - Affiliated with HKUST NLP Group, supervised by Professor Junxian He
- **2020 – 2024**: Bachelor of Engineering (B.Eng.), Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), Shanghai, China
  - Graduated in June 2024
  - Recipient of the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship

## Research Experience
- **February 2025 – Present**: Research Intern, MINIMAX
- **June 2024 – September 2024**: Research Intern, Tencent WXG
  - Advised by Zifei Shan
- **June 2023 – December 2023**: Research Intern, Shanghai AI Lab
  - Advised by Prof. Yu Cheng

## Research Interests
My research interests include:
- Large Language Model (LLM) Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLMs)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
- Core natural language processing and machine learning problems

## Skills
- Natural Language Processing (NLP) research
- Machine learning for foundation models
- LLM/VLM reasoning, analysis, and hallucination mitigation
- Verifiable reasoning data synthesis
- Model truthfulness and interpretability evaluation
